# Quality Gates

Use these gates after drafting and before delivering any audience-facing copy.

## Mandatory gates

| Gate | Pass question | Typical failure | Required fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Truth | Is every promise, feature, condition, price, date, and result supported? | Invented feature, hidden condition, unsupported guarantee | Restore the condition, remove the claim, or flag the missing fact |
| Meaning | Did the important distinction survive the rewrite? | Shorter copy that changes the offer or removes a limitation | Rebuild from the locked meaning, not from the wording |
| Three-second | Can the intended audience understand the point immediately? | Abstract headline, internal terminology, several ideas at once | Lead with the concrete situation, result, or action |
| Class-five | Can a person without specialist knowledge understand it? | Literal translations of marketing, product, analytics, or AI jargon | Explain what happens for the user |
| Speech | Does it sound natural when read aloud? | Translated English syntax, bureaucratic phrasing, unnatural formality | Rewrite as a real person would say it |
| Action | Is the next step obvious where an action is expected? | «ادامه»، «ارسال»، «بیشتر بدانید» without context | Name the immediate action or destination |
| Dignity | Is it simple without sounding childish, cheap, or patronizing? | Baby talk, unnecessary slang, over-explaining obvious points | Keep adult respect and remove performance |
| Mobile | Can it be scanned on a small screen? | Long paragraphs, six-line buttons, stacked qualifiers | Reduce ideas per block and protect hierarchy |
| Brand | Could this belong to any generic AI website? | Clichés, vague transformation language, template copy | Restore the project’s distinct point of view |

## Surface-specific gates

### Headline

Pass only when:

- it communicates one main idea
- it does not require the paragraph below to become understandable
- it avoids an internal category as the lead
- it does not promise more than the destination delivers
- its mobile wrap keeps the emphasis intact

### Button

Pass only when:

- the immediate result of tapping is predictable
- the label is specific enough without surrounding explanation
- it fits the available mobile width
- it remains accurate if the destination changes state

### Form question

Pass only when:

- the user knows what to enter or choose
- the reason for unusual information is clear
- required and optional fields are distinguishable
- the question does not expose internal qualification language

### Error

Pass only when it states:

1. what went wrong
2. what the user can do now

Never blame the user. Never expose internal error codes unless the audience genuinely needs them.

### Success

Pass only when it confirms:

1. what happened
2. what happens next

Do not add a promised response time unless operations guarantee it.

### Privacy and consent

Pass only when:

- the plain-language statement is technically true
- data categories are not hidden behind vague words such as «اطلاعات»
- the user’s choice is understandable
- legal completeness is preserved elsewhere when required

## Severity order

Fix failures in this order:

1. Truth
2. Meaning
3. Safety or legal meaning
4. Action
5. Three-second comprehension
6. Dignity
7. Mobile scanability
8. Brand distinction
9. Polish

Never improve style by weakening a higher-priority gate.

## Read-aloud test

Read the final Persian sentence aloud once.

Rewrite if:

- you run out of breath
- the verb arrives too late
- two clauses compete for attention
- the sentence sounds like translated English
- a real person would naturally use different words

## Anti-shortness test

Shorter is not automatically simpler.

Bad simplification:

«اطلاعاتت امنه.»

Why it fails:

- vague
- unsupported
- hides what data is meant

Better when accurate:

«اسم و شماره‌ای که در فرم می‌نویسی برای آمار سایت ارسال نمی‌شه.»

## Final decision

- If Truth or Meaning fails: stop and fix.
- If a necessary fact is missing: ask or flag it.
- If only style remains: deliver the strongest concise version.
