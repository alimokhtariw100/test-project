# Quality Gates

Use these gates before approving any audience-facing Persian copy. The goal is not merely shorter text. The goal is accurate meaning with less mental effort.

## Quick pass

A string is ready only when all mandatory gates pass:

1. Truth
2. Meaning
3. Three-second comprehension
4. Class-five comprehension
5. Natural speech
6. Clear action
7. Audience dignity
8. Mobile scanability
9. Brand specificity

For legal, medical, financial, privacy, payment, eligibility, pricing, or safety copy, Truth and Meaning are absolute blockers.

---

## 1. Truth gate

Ask:

- Is every factual statement supported by the product, source, or verified behavior?
- Does the CTA destination really exist and work?
- Is the promised next step available now?
- Are price, time, access, capacity, and conditions exact?
- Does the wording accidentally imply a guarantee?

Fail examples:

- Promising a personalized result when the product only shows a generic page
- Saying «همین الان دسترسی می‌گیری» when access is manual
- Saying «رایگان» when payment or a required purchase appears later
- Saying «بدون نیاز به تجربه» when prerequisite knowledge is required

Repair rule:

Change the copy or fix the product. Never use persuasive wording to hide a product gap.

---

## 2. Meaning gate

Ask:

- Which facts, conditions, or distinctions must survive the rewrite?
- Did simplification remove something that changes the user's decision?
- Could two materially different ideas now sound identical?

Bad simplification:

«اطلاعاتت رو بررسی می‌کنیم.»

When the real meaning is:

«شماره و توضیحت فقط برای بررسی درخواست و تماس با تو استفاده می‌شه.»

Shorter is not automatically clearer.

---

## 3. Three-second gate

Show only the string and ask:

- Can the intended audience say what this is about within three seconds?
- Can they identify the most important word without reading twice?
- Does the headline lead with the point, or with setup and jargon?

Fail signal:

The reader needs the next paragraph to decode the headline.

Repair tactics:

- Move the result or problem to the first line.
- Remove throat-clearing phrases.
- Replace an internal category with a user situation.
- Split two ideas into separate lines or layers.

---

## 4. Class-five gate

This is a knowledge test, not a tone test.

Ask:

- Would someone without marketing, product, analytics, AI, or UX vocabulary understand it?
- Does the sentence require knowing an internal term?
- Is a literal translation pretending to be simple Persian?

Do not make the voice childish. Keep adult dignity and accurate meaning.

Examples:

- `Diagnostic` → «ببین از کجا شروع کنی»
- `Lead form` → «درخواستت رو بفرست»
- `Activation` → «اولین قدمت رو بردار»

---

## 5. Speech gate

Read the text aloud.

Ask:

- Could the named person or brand naturally say this?
- Is the word order Persian, or translated English?
- Does it sound like a human helping another human?
- Are there stacked nouns that nobody uses in conversation?

Common failures:

- «بهره‌برداری از قابلیت‌های هوش مصنوعی»
- «در راستای تسهیل فرایند یادگیری»
- «تجربه شخصی‌سازی‌شده مبتنی بر نیاز»

Possible repairs:

- «از AI توی کارت استفاده کن.»
- «یادگیری رو ساده‌تر می‌کنیم.»
- «فقط چیزی رو ببین که به کارت می‌آد.»

---

## 6. Action gate

For actionable surfaces, ask:

- What happens immediately after the user taps or submits?
- Does the label describe that next step?
- Is the verb specific enough?
- Does the success message tell the user what happens next?

Stronger labels:

- «رایگان شروع کن»
- «آموزش رو ببین»
- «درخواست مشاوره»
- «شماره‌ت رو اصلاح کن»

Weaker labels:

- «بیشتر بدانید»
- «ارسال»
- «ادامه»
- «ثبت»

A generic label is acceptable only when the immediate context removes all ambiguity.

---

## 7. Dignity gate

Ask:

- Is the copy simple without treating the user as unintelligent?
- Is slang appropriate for this audience and moment?
- Does humor reduce shame, or make fun of the user?
- Does the copy preserve authority in payment, privacy, legal, health, and support contexts?

Avoid:

- fake friendliness
- forced emojis
- mocking mistakes
- baby talk
- overexplaining obvious controls
- motivational pressure disguised as help

---

## 8. Mobile gate

Review at the actual target width when possible.

Ask:

- Can the copy be scanned without becoming a wall?
- Is the main message visible before secondary explanation?
- Is the button label short enough to fit without shrinking?
- Are line breaks helping meaning rather than happening accidentally?
- Is one section trying to communicate too many ideas?

Useful defaults, not rigid limits:

- Button: usually 1–4 words
- Form label: usually one clear phrase
- Error: usually one short problem sentence plus one action sentence
- Hero: one central promise, one support line, one primary action
- Mobile paragraph: prefer 1–3 short sentences per block

---

## 9. Brand gate

Ask:

- Could this exact text belong to one hundred competitors?
- Does it express the project's point of view?
- Is the brand's signature word being repeated until it becomes empty?
- Does the copy sound like the founder or like a template?

Generic:

«قدرت هوش مصنوعی را در مسیر رشد خود آزاد کنید.»

Specific:

«قرار نیست همه‌ی هوش مصنوعی رو یاد بگیری؛ فقط چیزی که به درد تو می‌خوره.»

Do not force brand slogans into every section. A signature becomes weaker when repeated without purpose.

---

## Scoring rubric for audits

Score each gate:

- 2 = passes clearly
- 1 = understandable but weak, generic, or slightly risky
- 0 = fails or cannot be verified

Maximum: 18

Interpretation:

- 16–18: ready, assuming product verification passes
- 12–15: revise before high-traffic use
- 8–11: substantial rewrite needed
- 0–7: the audience likely misunderstands or distrusts it

Any score of 0 in Truth or Meaning blocks approval regardless of total score.

## Final question

Before approval, complete this sentence in plain Persian:

«بعد از دیدن این متن، مخاطب می‌فهمه که ______ و قدم بعدیش ______ است.»

If this cannot be completed precisely, the copy is not ready.
